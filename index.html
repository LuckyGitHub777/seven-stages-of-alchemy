About

Alchemy has often been read as work on matter and, at the same time, as a mirror for inner transformation. These seven stages follow a widely used modern form of that reading.

The wording, figures and sound are original. Independent work, not affiliated with any person or organization. The planet and metal correspondences follow one commonly presented scheme, and sources vary.

No recordings are used. The optional drone is generated live in your browser.
